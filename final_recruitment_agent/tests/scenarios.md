# Demo scenarios

## 1. Natural language matching
**User:** Find me candidates with React and 3+ years experience.
**Show:** requirements, top 5, score breakdown, must-have pass/fail.

## 2. Side-by-side comparison
**User:** Compare the top 3 matches side by side.
**CLI:** `/compare Name A,Name B,Name C`

## 3. Explain ranking
**User:** Why did Candidate A rank higher than Candidate B?
**Show:** semantic score, keyword score, matched skills, missing must-haves, experience and excerpts.

## 4. Iterative refinement
**User:** Must have TypeScript and 5+ years experience.
**Show:** re-ranking and changed qualification status.

## 5. Multi-round screening
**User:** Run final screening.
**CLI:** `/screen`
**Show:** top 10, deep-analysis scores, finalists, final HIRE/HOLD.
